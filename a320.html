How the trainer works

Electrical

Two batteries, two engine generators (IDG), the APU generator and external power feed AC BUS 1 and 2; each generator normally feeds its own side and one source can feed both through the bus tie. AC ESS comes from AC BUS 1 (ALTN: AC BUS 2). With batteries only on the ground the screens stay dark: check both batteries above 25.5 V, then use external power or the APU.

APU and engine start

APU MASTER SW opens the air intake flap; START spins the APU up to AVAIL in about a minute. With the APU BLEED on and the mode selector at IGN/START, ENG MASTER ON starts an engine automatically: starter valve, ignition at 16 % N2, fuel at 22 %, light-off, starter cut-out at 50 %, idle at about 59 % N2. The packs close during a start.

Hydraulics

Green (engine 1 pump), blue (electric pump, on when an engine runs) and yellow (engine 2 pump, electric pump). The PTU joins green and yellow when their pressures differ by more than 500 psi; it is inhibited between the first and the second engine start with the parking brake on.

ECAM

Warnings (red, MASTER WARN and the continuous chime) and cautions (amber, MASTER CAUT and a single chime) show on the E/WD with their actions in blue; an action disappears when the system senses it done. CLR goes to the next alert, RCL brings them back, STS shows the status page. T.O and LDG memos, the right memo and the SD page follow the flight phase.

Fly-by-wire and auto flight

Normal law: load factor demand with automatic trim, roll rate demand with bank hold up to 33°, protections for pitch attitude, bank, high angle of attack (alpha floor) and high speed, and the flare mode below 50 ft. FCU: pull a knob for selected guidance, push it for managed. The FMA shows what the A/THR, the vertical and the lateral modes are doing.

What is simplified

The flight model is a teaching model tuned to typical A320neo numbers; the FMS knows the practice area only (field, fixes and the ILS 36), managed climb and descent use the FCU altitude, and some panels (cargo, cabin, communications) are pictures only. Our own drawings of the display formats.
