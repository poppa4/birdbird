Aerodrome and AIP

Type an ICAO code and go straight to the aerodrome's official AIP and charts, in the edition in force today. Press Enter and the AIP opens. Next to it: NOTAM, the weather now and daylight.

Runways, declared distances, frequencies and charts are taken from the AIP itself, never copied into NORDAP. The AIP, NOTAM and the official weather briefing are the authority.
