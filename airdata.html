Air data: pressure, altitude and airspeed

Two pressures run the basic flight instruments. The static ports feel the still air around you, the pitot tube feels the air rammed into it. From them the altimeter, the airspeed indicator and the VSI work out height, speed and climb. See what each setting means, where the altimeter lies, how IAS becomes TAS, and what a blocked tube does.
