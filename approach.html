2 · Numbers

Rate of descent and check altitudes

Rules of thumb: on a 3° path you lose about 318 ft per NM, and the rate of descent is about groundspeed × 5 (more exactly × 5.3). Half the groundspeed × 10 gives the same answer.

Inside the ILS signal

The needles do not measure distance. They compare two tones, 90 Hz and 150 Hz, and show the difference in depth of modulation (DDM). Move the aircraft through the beams, find the false glide paths, see what an aircraft in the wrong place does to the signal, and why the low categories need autoland.

Segments and minima

Teaching model. Full-scale deflections are typical values (localizer about ±2.5°, glide path about ±0.7°, RNP final ±0.3 NM, shown here on a 2-dot display; on the classic 5-dot display one dot is about 0.5° on the localizer and 0.14° on the glide path) and vary by installation and avionics. The A320 PFD is a simplified drawing; real displays differ by standard and software. ILS: on-course modulation 20 % per tone on the localizer and 40 % on the glide path; full scale is DDM 0.155 (localizer) and 0.175 (glide path); the glide path lobes are modelled as DDM ∝ sin(π·angle/3°). Protected-area bends and the cockpit cut-off (20°, pitch +2.5°) are typical illustrative values. CAT definitions follow ICAO Annex 6; operator and aerodrome minima can be higher. Fly the published procedure and your aircraft’s limitations.
