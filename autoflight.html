Automation and warnings: autopilot, TCAS and EGPWS

The autopilot flies what the mode annunciator says, not what you meant. Compare a simple two-axis autopilot in a light aircraft with the flight director, autopilot and autothrust of a jet, see how modes arm, capture and revert, and then how TCAS and EGPWS decide when to warn you, and what you do when they do.
