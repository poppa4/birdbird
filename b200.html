Flight controls

Drag either control wheel on the panel, use the keyboard, a gamepad, touch controls or this pad.

How to use the panel
Zoom

Tap an area to zoom to it; pinch or scroll to zoom in and out, drag to move when zoomed. ← Back, Esc or a double tap returns to the whole panel. Keys 0–9 jump between areas.

Switches

Click a toggle to flip it. Three-position switches: click the top or bottom half to select that position, the middle to return to the centre. Guarded switches: the first click lifts the guard.

Levers

Drag the power, propeller and condition levers up and down; they snap to their detents. Beta and reverse (below IDLE) work on the ground only. The scroll wheel and the arrow keys move a lever in small steps.

Knobs

Scroll on a knob or drag across it. HDG and CRS set the heading bug and the course on the HSI, ALT SEL the altitude alerter, CABIN ALT and RATE the pressurisation controller.

Failures

Engines

Flame out an engine at any time. With the autofeather armed and both power levers forward, the failed propeller feathers itself.

Pressurisation

Lose the cabin pressure. The cabin climbs towards the aircraft altitude; ALT WARN comes on at 12,500 ft cabin altitude.

Ground power

Plug a ground power unit in on the ramp (EXT PWR light). With the battery ON, EXT PWR — ON puts it on line.

How the model works

Engine start

IGN & START ON runs the starter and the igniters (IGNITION ON). Introduce fuel with the condition lever at LOW IDLE at a stabilised 12 % N1 or more. Fuel too early, a weak start or high idle gives a hotter start; above 1,000 °C ITT is a hot start. Starter OFF at about 50 % N1, then GEN RESET and ON.

Autofeather

Armed with the switch at ARM and both power levers above about 90 % N1: two green AUTOFEATHER lights. Below 400 ft-lb on one engine the other side disarms; below 200 ft-lb the failing propeller feathers. TEST bypasses the power-lever switches so you can check it at low power.

Propellers

The propeller lever sets the governed rpm, 1,600 to 2,000. Pulled through the gate it feathers the propeller. Below IDLE the power lever controls the blade angle directly: beta for taxi, reverse for braking.

Pressurisation

Bleed air from each engine pressurises the cabin. The controller holds the selected cabin altitude until the differential reaches about 6.5 psi. Dump, no bleed air or a leak lets the cabin climb to the aircraft altitude.

Annunciators

Red is a warning, yellow a caution, green an advisory. A new red or yellow light flashes MASTER WARNING or MASTER CAUTION on the glareshield; press it to acknowledge.

What is simplified

The flight model is a generic twin tuned to B200-like numbers. Gauges, limits and procedures are typical values for learning: no serial-number differences, no AC bus, no fuel transfer.
