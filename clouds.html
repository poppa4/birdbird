Clouds, stability and fog

Almost all weather starts the same way: air is cooled until it reaches its dew point. Lift a parcel of air and see whether it keeps rising into a cumulus or a thunderstorm, or sinks back and spreads out as a flat layer. Then cool a clear, calm night and watch fog form.
