Powerplant: piston, turboprop and jet

Every aircraft engine does the same four things to air: suck it in, squeeze it, burn fuel in it and blow it out. A piston engine does them one after another in the same cylinder; a gas turbine does them all at once, each in its own part of the engine. What the hot gas then drives makes the difference: a propeller through a shaft (piston and turboprop) or a jet of air straight out of the back (turbojet and turbofan). See each one work, read its gauges, compare them at the same height and speed, and start them.
