In-flight fuel monitoring

At every fuel check the crew compares the fuel on board with the plan and predicts the fuel at landing. The prediction, not the fuel on board now, drives the decisions: continue, re-plan, divert, commit to the destination, and tell ATC.

Teaching model of an A320ceo-sized aircraft: MTOM 78,000 kg, MLM 64,500 kg, MZFM 62,500 kg, DOM 42,500 kg, usable fuel about 18,700 kg (typical). Fuel flows, trip fuel, alternate fuel, the cruise level model and the tankering penalty are simplified typical values, not FCOM or flight-planning-system data. The fuel rules follow the ideas of the EASA basic fuel scheme (CAT.OP.MPA.181 and its AMC) in simplified form; the operator's approved fuel scheme, aerodrome selection policy and the current regulations always apply.
