How it works

1 · Build

Pick your cockpit: one of NORDAP's lifelike flight decks where every control has its name, or your own photo or PDF page. On your own picture you can give a control a name once (open the step with ⋯); after that it works like a built-in control: add it by name, a tap snaps to it, and you can drill finding it. Choose the PF or PM layer and tap the controls in order. Paste your SOP to get all the items at once, then place them one by one. Add checklist calls, outside events (ATC clearance, fuel figures, cabin secure), steps that wait for the other pilot, and notes on the panels.

2 · Watch

The flow plays step by step with its path drawn between the controls, one role or both, with the checklist calls on screen and the items read aloud if you like.

3 · Practise

Three levels. Learn it: tap each step in order, with hints when you need them. From memory: tap the whole flow with nothing shown, then see your path over the real one, with steps out of order and steps you missed marked. Check ride: one go, no hints, scored. Flashcards ask for the controls in random order, and on NORDAP's cockpits you can learn the whole panel.

4 · Together

Open a room and send the link or code to your partner on another device. One takes PF, the other PM. The flow runs in order across both roles: you find your steps, you see your partner's taps on your screen, and checklist calls pop up for both. Scores side by side at the end.

NORDAP's cockpit drawings show the panels in their usual places, simplified; layouts differ between variants and operators. The example flows are scan patterns, not SOPs: use your operator's or school's SOP. Your flows and pictures are kept in this browser only. A shared link carries the flow, never your photo.
