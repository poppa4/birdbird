How it works

With help

One step at a time, in the order you would do it in the cockpit. Stuck? Hint shows the keys to press and what to type, and lights the keys on the CDU.

On your own

The same task without the steps: only the clearance and the paperwork. Set it up your way, then check your setup and see what is missing.

Find the field

The trainer names a field, for example “Enter the ZFW”. Find the page and press the right line key. This builds the muscle memory.

The keyboard

Type letters and numbers on your computer keyboard, Backspace is CLR and Delete is DEL. After you tap the CDU, the arrow keys work as PREV PAGE and NEXT PAGE. Hold CLR to clear the whole scratchpad.

MOD, EXEC and the ND

A change to the active route shows MOD in the title and lights EXEC; nothing changes until you press it. The ND has MAP (track up, as you fly) and PLAN (north up, centred on the <CTR> waypoint on LEGS; STEP moves it). The active route is magenta, a MOD a white dashed line, an inactive route cyan and FIX rings and radials green. In flight, ▶ on the MCP flies the aircraft: it follows the route in LNAV, sequences the waypoints and flies the hold until EXIT ARMED, and holds a heading in HDG SEL. Boxes must be filled, small letters are computed values and large letters are your own entries.

A generic, simplified 737 FMC for learning: not every page and function is modelled, and it is not an aircraft or simulator FMC. The navigation data is fictional (Nordvik XNDV, Skarhamn XSKH and Fjellby XFJB) and the weights, speeds and fuel figures are teaching values. Not for navigation or for operational use: follow your operator's FCOM and SOP.
