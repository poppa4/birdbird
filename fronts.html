Global circulation and climate

The sun heats the tropics most. Rising air at the equator and sinking air around 30° form the Hadley cells; the Ferrel and polar cells complete the picture towards the poles. Together they set the pressure belts, the prevailing winds, the ITCZ and where the jet streams sit. Move the latitude and the month and watch the belts follow the sun.
