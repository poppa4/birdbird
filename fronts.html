Fronts and air masses

Where two air masses meet, the warmer air is lifted and the weather happens. Fly through a warm front, a cold front or an occlusion and see the cloud, rain, freezing level, icing and turbulence where you are, with the wind, temperature and QNH changing as you pass. Then watch a frontal depression grow from a wave to an occlusion.
