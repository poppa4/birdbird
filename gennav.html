General navigation

Heading is where the nose points, track is where you actually go, and the wind makes the difference. Solve the wind triangle the way a CRP-5 does, turn it into a magnetic heading, a time and the fuel for the leg, and get back on track with the 1 in 60 rule. In Deep dive, follow great circles across the globe and onto charts, and find the PET and the PNR.
