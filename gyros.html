Attitude and heading: compass, gyros and inertial systems

Which way am I pointing, and which way is up? The magnetic compass answers the first question but lies when you turn or accelerate. Spinning gyros answer both, but drift and topple. Inertial systems and AHRS do it with lasers and silicon. See each one work, and see where it fails.
