Icing and turbulence

One cross-section of the atmosphere with the two hazards you meet on almost every flight. Fly through cloud at your chosen temperature and drop size and watch ice build on the wing. Then meet mountain waves, rotors and clear air turbulence near the jet stream.

Learning goalPredict where ice forms and what kind, explain what it does to the stall, and know where to expect turbulence over mountains and near the jet stream, and how bad it can get.
