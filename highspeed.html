1 · Understand

Two limits: drag at the slow end, Mach at the fast end

At the slow end the limit is drag and the stall. Drag is parasite drag, which grows with speed, plus induced drag, the price of lift, which falls with speed. Below the bottom of the curve, the minimum drag speed, the speed is unstable. At the fast end the limit is the Mach number: past the critical Mach number part of the flow over the wing goes supersonic, a shock wave forms, and wave drag and buffet follow.

Deep dive · the numbers behind it

Speed of sound. a = 38.94 √T kt, with T in kelvin: 661 kt at 15 °C (ISA sea level), 573 kt at −56.5 °C (ISA above the tropopause). It depends only on temperature. Mach number M = TAS / a.

Minimum drag and minimum power. With a parabolic drag polar, induced and parasite drag are equal at Vmd. The minimum power speed Vmp is about 0.76 Vmd (Vmd / 1.32). For a jet, Vmd gives the longest endurance.

Typical values. An airliner wing reaches its critical Mach at about M 0.70–0.75 in cruise; the A320 MMO is M 0.82. Drag divergence follows a few hundredths of Mach above Mcrit.
