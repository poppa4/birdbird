Drag and speed stability

Total drag is the sum of induced drag, which falls with speed, and parasite drag, which rises with it. The bottom of the curve is the minimum drag speed. For the aerofoil and the stall itself, see the wind tunnel.

Mach and compressibility

The speed of sound depends only on temperature, so it falls as you climb. The airspeed indicator works on dynamic pressure, the wing feels Mach number. A climb at constant IAS therefore ends in a climb at constant Mach.

The machmeter

The machmeter needs no thermometer. It compares the dynamic pressure with the static pressure, and that ratio depends only on the Mach number.

The swept wing

Sweep makes the wing see only the part of the airflow that is at right angles to the leading edge. That delays the shock waves, but it also sends air outboard along the span, and that has consequences at low speed and in yaw.

High-altitude stall and upset recovery

In normal law the A320 protects its angle of attack. After an air data failure, in alternate or direct law, it can stall like any other aircraft, and at high level the recovery is slow and costs thousands of feet. Start a stall at altitude and choose how you recover.
