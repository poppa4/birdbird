Holding and instrument approach

The end of every IFR flight: wait in a hold, then fly the approach down to the minimum, and go around if you cannot see the runway. Fly hold entries and holds in wind at the FLS VOR, then an ILS, a non-precision CDFA and a missed approach to runway 17 at Bergen, with the map, the profile and the instruments side by side.

    Model and limitations. Training model, not for navigation. Holding rules from ICAO Doc 8168 (PANS-OPS) Vol I: entry sectors with 5° flexibility, 1 min outbound at or below 14 000 ft and 1.5 min above, turns at 25° bank or rate one, whichever needs less bank. The hold is flown at constant TAS; outbound it uses three times the inbound drift correction and an outbound time that counts the along-track drift in the turns, so the inbound leg comes out at about the planned time. The approach is illustrative, not the published Bergen chart: IAF 14 NM, IF 10 NM, FAP where 2000 ft meets the 3° path, DA 370 ft (DH 200 ft), localiser-only MDA 600 ft, missed approach climbing straight ahead at 4 % to 3000 ft and back to a hold at FLS. FLS position and the ILS RWY 17 geometry are the radio navigation simulator’s (OurAirports data, approximate threshold); relief and coastline from Mapzen/AWS Terrain Tiles. No magnetic variation, no wind on the approach, constant ground speed, no instrument errors. Classifications from ICAO Annex 6; operator and aerodrome minima can be higher.
