    What the trainer models

    Rate-one turns (at most 25° of bank), climbs and descents to the selected altitude at the selected rate, true airspeed rising about 2 % per 1000 ft, and wind that is steady or gusty and variable. The heading bug works like a simple autopilot: the aircraft turns towards it, the way you turned the bug. Magnetic variation is set to zero so the map and the instruments agree.

    Limits and tolerances

    Scores use the EASA IR skill test limits: ±100 ft, tracking within ±5°, half-scale on the ILS, never below the MDA. Holding follows ICAO PANS-OPS: right turns unless told otherwise, 1-minute legs at or below 14 000 ft, entries decided by heading with 5° of freedom either side of a sector line. The procedure turn uses the Cat A/B timing of 1 minute.

    Real and practice data

    Bergen and Oslo use real navaid positions and frequencies (OurAirports) for training only. The procedures flown there are illustrative, not the published charts. The practice area is fictional: Holmvik, Kvist, Fjell and Ravn do not exist. Never use this trainer for navigation.
