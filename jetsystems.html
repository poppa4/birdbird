Aircraft systems: hydraulics, electrics, fuel, flight controls and landing gear

Every aircraft has the same jobs to do: make electricity, move heavy things, get fuel to the engines, connect the pilot to the control surfaces, and lower the gear. How it does them changes with size. A light piston twin does them with alternators, cables and an electric gear motor; a turboprop adds a hydraulic system and inverters; a jet transport has three hydraulic systems, AC power and fly-by-wire. See each one live, fail parts of it, and see what still works.
