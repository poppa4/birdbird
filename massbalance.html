Masses and limits

Every flight is checked against three structural limits: maximum zero fuel mass, maximum take-off mass and maximum landing mass. Each one allows a different traffic load, and the lowest of the three is the one you can actually carry.

Centre of gravity

The CG is the total moment divided by the total mass. On a transport aircraft it is given in percent of the mean aerodynamic chord (% MAC), and load sheets add moments as an index to keep the numbers small. Why the CG limits exist aerodynamically is covered in the stability lab.
