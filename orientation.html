1 · How you know which way is up

The three senses

≈ 80 %Vision

The most reliable sense, as long as you can see a real horizon. In cloud, at night or over featureless terrain it is gone, and the instruments must replace it.

Inner earVestibular system

The semicircular canals sense rotation, but only changes in rotation. The otoliths sense linear acceleration and gravity, but cannot tell the two apart.

BodyProprioception, “seat of the pants”

Pressure on the seat and in the muscles. In a coordinated turn the force always points down through the seat, so it feels like level flight.
