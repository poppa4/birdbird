Pitot-static system

Three instruments run on two pressures: total pressure from the pitot tube and static pressure from the static ports. Block one of them, with ice, water or an insect, and climb or descend to see exactly how each instrument lies.

Unreliable airspeed on a jet

A jet such as the A320 has three pitot-static sets, one for each air data reference (ADR). Three is not the same as independent: ice crystals at cruise level can block several probes in the same minute. Ice the probes, watch the flight control computers vote, then fly the aircraft on pitch and thrust.

Flying pitch and thrust

Attitude and thrust do not need the pitot. A known pitch with a known thrust gives a known speed, within a band that is safe for any mass. That is the logic of the memory targets, and of the tables you fly once the aircraft is under control.
