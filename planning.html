2 · EASA Part-NCO

Fuel

For non-commercial flights in a non-complex aeroplane (Part-NCO), plan to reach the destination and still land with the final reserve: 10 minutes for a local day VFR flight in sight of the aerodrome, 30 minutes for other day VFR, and 45 minutes at night or IFR, where IFR also needs fuel to the alternate. The rule also requires fuel for reasonably expected contingencies and delays (AMC1 NCO.OP.125).
