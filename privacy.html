The short version

  • An account is optional. Without one, your progress and settings are stored in your own browser only.
  • With an account, your progress, briefing packs, routes and settings are also saved on NORDAP’s server, so they follow you to your other devices. You can delete the account at any time.
  • Documents you add, such as an AFM, QRH or SOP, are processed and stored in your own browser. A private copy is kept in your account only if you are signed in and cloud storage for documents is switched on.
  • Messages you send through a form, and flows you publish in the flow library, are stored on NORDAP’s server. See Forms and sharing.
  • AI features only send something when you use them. On nordap.eu the requests go to Cloudflare Workers AI (or Anthropic, if that is switched on); nothing of the question or the answer is stored.
  • No analytics, no tracking cookies and no advertising. The only cookie is the sign-in cookie, and only if you sign in.
  • Fonts are served from NORDAP itself, not from Google or another third party.

What is stored, and where

NORDAP uses two kinds of storage that belong to your browser: local storage for small settings and progress, and IndexedDB for documents and larger files. Neither is sent to a NORDAP server, except document files when you are signed in and cloud storage for documents is switched on (see Accounts and sync).

Without an account, nothing is stored on a NORDAP server, so NORDAP cannot see, recover or delete that data for you. Clearing it is in your hands, see below.

Delete your account. Go to My page, then Account, and choose Delete account. The account, its passkeys, sessions, all synced data and any document files in cloud storage are deleted from the server straight away. You can also tick a box to clear this browser at the same time.

Sign out. Signing out ends the session on the server. Your data stays in that browser until you clear it.

AI features

Flight Intelligence, the oral exam in the type rating trainer and some training exercises use an AI model. On nordap.eu the request is answered by Cloudflare Workers AI (Cloudflare, Inc.), which runs an open model on Cloudflare’s network and does not use the requests to train models. If NORDAP switches to Anthropic’s Claude for answers, the request goes to Anthropic instead, which does not train on requests sent through its API either. When NORDAP runs inside Claude, the request goes through your own Claude account.

A request contains your question and the passages from your documents that are needed to answer it, not the whole library. NORDAP does not store or log the question or the answer. It only counts how many questions are asked per day: for an account, under your account; without an account, under a one-way code made from your IP address with a key that changes every day, never the IP address itself. The counts are deleted after five weeks.

Where no AI is available, the AI features are switched off and nothing is sent.

Before it changesIf the company that answers the AI requests changes, this page will be updated before the change is switched on.

To stop misuse, each of these counts how often it is used per day under a one-way code made from your IP address, never the address itself.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

NORDAP does not use analytics or tracking tools and shows no advertising. No tracking pixels, no social media widgets. The only cookie is the sign-in cookie, set when you sign in to an account and removed when you sign out. It is needed for the account to work and is never used to follow you.

The fonts are stored with the site and loaded from it, so opening a page does not send your address to a font service.

Like any website, the server that delivers the pages may keep ordinary technical logs, such as the time of a request and an IP address, for operation and security. NORDAP does not use them to follow you.

How to clear your data

Clear everything in this browser

Removes progress, settings, saved plans, setups and documents that NORDAP has stored in this browser. It cannot be undone. If you are signed in, you are signed out first, and the copy in your account is kept.

Or use your browser

  1. Open your browser's settings and find privacy, or site data.
  2. Look up the NORDAP site and choose to delete its data (sometimes called cookies and site data).
  3. To remove everything at once, clear browsing data for all time, including cookies and site data.

Data saved to your NORDAP account is removed by deleting the account on My page. Data kept with your Claude account, when NORDAP runs inside Claude, is removed by deleting the saved items inside NORDAP.

Who is responsible, and your rights

NORDAP is run by Daniel Nilsson, a private person in Norway, who is responsible for the processing of personal data described on this page (the data controller).

Why the data is processed: for an account and the features you choose to use, to provide the service you ask for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)). For forms, to answer you, and for sign-in counters and server logs, to keep NORDAP secure and working (legitimate interest, article 6(1)(f)).

Cloudflare (hosting, database, file storage and the free AI model), Resend (e-mail) and, if switched on, Anthropic (AI answers) process data on NORDAP’s behalf. Some of them are based in the United States; transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU standard contractual clauses.

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can object to processing. Much of it you can do yourself on My page. For anything else, write through the contact form and choose Privacy.

You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.no.
