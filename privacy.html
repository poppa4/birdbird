Privacy

What Birdbird stores, where it is stored, and how to remove it, in plain language.

The short version

  • Birdbird has no user accounts yet. You do not sign up or log in.
  • Your progress, ranks and settings are stored in your own browser.
  • Documents you add, such as an AFM, QRH or SOP, are processed and stored in your own browser.
  • AI features send requests through your own Claude account, and only when you use them.
  • No analytics, no tracking cookies and no advertising.
  • Fonts are served from Birdbird itself, not from Google or another third party.

What is stored, and where

Birdbird uses two kinds of storage that belong to your browser: local storage for small settings and progress, and IndexedDB for documents and larger files. Neither is sent to a Birdbird server.

When Birdbird runs inside ClaudeIf you open Birdbird as an app inside Claude, saved briefing packs, aircraft, instructor setups and sessions are also kept in private storage tied to your own Claude account, so they follow you between devices. Only you can see them. Your documents are not copied there; they stay in the browser.

Because nothing is stored on a Birdbird server, Birdbird cannot see, recover or delete your data for you. Clearing it is in your hands, see below.

AI features

Flight Intelligence, the oral exam in the type rating trainer and some training exercises use an AI model. When Birdbird runs inside Claude and you use one of these features, the request goes through your own Claude account.

A request contains your question and the passages from your documents that are needed to answer it, not the whole library. Anthropic's terms and privacy policy for your Claude account apply to those requests.

Where no AI is available, the AI features are switched off and nothing is sent.

Before it changesIf Birdbird later offers AI on its own domain, without a Claude account, this page will be updated before that service is switched on, and it will say who processes the requests.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

Birdbird does not set cookies, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and shows no advertising. No tracking pixels, no social media widgets.

The fonts are stored with the site and loaded from it, so opening a page does not send your address to a font service.

Like any website, the server that delivers the pages may keep ordinary technical logs, such as the time of a request and an IP address, for operation and security. Birdbird does not use them to follow you.

How to clear your data

Clear everything in this browser

Removes progress, settings, saved plans, setups and documents that Birdbird has stored in this browser. It cannot be undone.

Or use your browser

  1. Open your browser's settings and find privacy, or site data.
  2. Look up the Birdbird site and choose to delete its data (sometimes called cookies and site data).
  3. To remove everything at once, clear browsing data for all time, including cookies and site data.

Data kept with your Claude account, when Birdbird runs inside Claude, is removed by deleting the saved items inside Birdbird.

Changes to this page

Accounts, school features or a hosted AI service would change what is stored and where. This page will be updated before any of that is switched on.
