Engine failure and minimum control speeds

Lose an engine on a light twin and on the A320 and slow down until the rudder runs out. That speed is VMCA. See why one engine is critical, what bank, feathering, power and CG do, how a propeller blade works as a wing, and how CS-25 turns VMC into VMCG, VMCL and the limits on V1, VR, V2 and VREF.
