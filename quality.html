Who writes and checks

NORDAP is built and written by one commercial pilot, with AI tools for the programming. There is no editorial staff and no panel of instructors.

Nothing on the site has been reviewed by an instructor or a subject expert outside NORDAP yet. When that happens, the page concerned will say who reviewed it and when.

What is checked, and how

  • Rules and phraseology are written from the published regulation, and the page names the rule.
  • Data from outside sources is shown with its source. The sources and their licences are listed on the data sources page.
  • The link to each country's official AIP is tested automatically every week.
  • Weather sources and the live site are tested automatically.
  • Every change to the code runs the automated tests before it is published. They test that the pages work, not that the content is right.
  • Control names in the flow trainer follow the label on the picture. Some have also been compared with public references.

What has not been checked

  • The flight models have not been compared with flight-test data or a certified model.
  • Gate to gate, the IFR trainer and the briefings have not been reviewed by an instructor.
  • The labs have not been mapped to the EASA learning objectives one by one.
  • No study has measured whether the tools improve learning. NORDAP makes no such claim.
  • The 3D cockpits and the maps have not been tested on every device.

What each tool is built on and what it leaves out is listed in the model profiles. Model profiles

Found an error?

The flow trainer has a button for a wrong or missing control name. For everything else, use Report an error, here or at the bottom of every page. A confirmed error is corrected and entered in the change log.
