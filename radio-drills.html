3 · Reference

Phraseology

Always read back

Route clearances. Clearances to enter, land on, take off from, cross, taxi on or backtrack a runway, and hold short of it. Runway in use, altimeter settings (QNH), squawk codes, levels, headings and speeds, transition levels and frequency changes. End the readback with your callsign.

First call

Who you call, who you are, where you are, what you want. Example: “Birdbird Tower, LN-NAE, DA42, 10 miles south, 2000 feet, information Bravo, request joining for landing.”

Words to avoid

Say “ready for departure”, not “ready for take-off” (take-off is used only in the clearance). Say “say again”, not “repeat”. Say “affirm”, not “yes” or “affirmative”. No “over and out”.
