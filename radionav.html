What to remember

Check

Test yourself

Teaching model with no magnetic variation and no wind: headings, tracks and bearings are all the same reference. VOR full scale is drawn as ±10°: on the ND scale, 2 dots each side, one dot is 5°. Radar: echo strength scales with 1/R⁴ for a primary return and 1/R² for a transponder reply; detection ranges, the radar antenna height (100 ft) and the screen labels are typical, simplified values.
