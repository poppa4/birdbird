Thunderstorms and wind shear

A cumulonimbus contains almost every hazard in aviation at once: severe turbulence, icing, hail, lightning, microbursts and gust fronts. Watch one grow, mature and die, fly around a cell on the radar and see where the danger reaches, then fly an approach through a microburst.

    Airborne weather radar

    The radar sends a narrow beam and shows how much energy comes back from precipitation. What you see depends on where the beam is: its tilt, how wide it has become at that range, and whether a strong cell in front has already absorbed the energy. Cruise towards two cells and find the tilt that shows the threat as it is.
