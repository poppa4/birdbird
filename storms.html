Thunderstorms and wind shear

A cumulonimbus holds almost every hazard in aviation at once: severe turbulence, icing, hail, lightning, gust fronts and microbursts. Grow a storm from a cumulus to a mature cell and watch it die, fly an approach through its microburst, and find it on the weather radar, including what the radar does not show you.
