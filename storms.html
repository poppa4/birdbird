Thunderstorms and wind shear

A cumulonimbus holds almost every hazard in aviation at once: severe turbulence, icing, hail, lightning, gust fronts and microbursts. Grow one in 3D from a cumulus to a mature storm and watch it die, look inside at the air that drives it, fly an approach through its microburst, and aim the weather radar at it.
