How it is worked out

NORDAP computes the times itself from the solar position (the NOAA equations), for a sea-level horizon and standard refraction: sunrise and sunset when the centre of the sun is 0.833° below the horizon, civil twilight at 6°, nautical at 12° and astronomical at 18°. The times are normally within a minute of the almanac. Terrain, such as mountains or a fjord, can hide the sun well after sunrise and before sunset. For flight planning, check the official sources your operator requires.
